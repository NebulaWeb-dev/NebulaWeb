How it works

A clean three-step orbit from idea to launch.

No corporate maze. Just a focused build with checkpoints where they matter. Nothing goes live until you're satisfied.

Phase 01
Signal scan

Quick call + questionnaire to map your goals, audience, and existing brand. We decide what actually needs to be on the site and what can wait.

Output: one-page build brief + site map.
Phase 02
Interface build

I design and develop the core pages, then walk you through an interactive preview. We tighten copy, tweak visuals, and sharpen CTAs.

Output: live prototype ready for integration.
Phase 03
Launch & refine

Connect the domain, plug in automations, and flip the switch. After launch we do a light analytics pass and adjust what isn’t pulling its weight.

Output: live site + simple performance snapshot.
